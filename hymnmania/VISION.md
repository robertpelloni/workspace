# HymnMania Vision

HymnMania is an automated pipeline designed to take classic Christian hymns and classical pieces (represented as MIDI files), render them at various tempo multipliers, upload them to Suno AI to generate modern electronic music covers (such as Psytrance, Gabber, and Japanese Hardcore Techno), render dynamic visualizer videos using projectM/MilkDrop and Magnific AI, and publish the results automatically to YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

## Core Pillars
1. **Faithful Melodic Continuity**: Ensuring Suno covers are actual covers (not loosely inspired generations) by uploading the speed-modified hymn audio as original tracks and using Suno's "Remix/Cover" feature.
2. **High-Intensity Electronic Genres**: Conditioning generations on specific underground electronic music styles (11 genres) to create high-value, highly engaging musical contrasts.
3. **Automated Visual Compositing**: Harnessing real-time MilkDrop visualizers synced with the audio, Magnific AI-generated video clips, and FFmpeg muxing into high-definition video files.
4. **Seamless Social Distribution**: Automated YouTube uploading with standardized titles/descriptions, Facebook posting with video preview cards, and Instagram templates.
5. **Consolidated System Control**: Single-page local dashboard controlled by a system tray icon for monitoring, configuration, and manual overrides.
