  • 🗂️
    Workspace Presets: Save your favorite groups of engines and switch between them with one click. For eg. Create a preset for "Shopping" with Amazon, AliExpress, Walmart, and eBay & launch these all in 1 click when needed
  • 🖱️
    Dynamic Context Menus: Your workspaces now automatically appear in the right-click menu! Highlight text and search directly within your custom "Shopping", "Dev", or "AI" workspaces.
  • ↕️
    Syncronized Scrolling: You can enable syncronized scrolling in settings to scroll all the results from multiple windows, at once! Can also choose on basis of scroll position (in pixels) or scroll percentage.

    Better Engine Picking: The engine selector now has a built-in search and category tabs (Shopping, Dev, AI, etc.) to help you find what you need instantly.
